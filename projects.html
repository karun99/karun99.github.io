System Architecture

Engineering the next generation of Agentic Workflows and Educational Intelligence.

Open-source AI projects built by Sai Karun Nandipati. — This page is part of the official site of Sai Karun Nandipati, Assistant Professor in the Department of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at PB Siddhartha College of Arts and Science, India; an AI researcher and fullstack engineer working on Agentic AI, multi-agent systems, Generative AI, RAG and Human-AI Symbiosis, and the author of more than 30 books on artificial intelligence. His projects include S-AI, a multi-agent swarm framework published on npm as @saikarun/s-ai, and Collabuild, a research-paper-to-prototype pipeline. He also works as a Content Writer at Yes&Yes Publications; responsibilities include content drafting, data writing, ensuring data accuracy. More about Sai Karun Nandipati · AI answers & FAQ
