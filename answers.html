Technical SEO/AEO

Answer-engine optimisation is mostly the discipline of stating facts in plain declarative sentences, close to the top of the page, and repeating them in a form a machine can parse. This site does that in five layers.

1 · Answer-shaped prose

The first paragraph on every page is a self-contained answer. Questions appear as headings and are answered in 40–60 words, which is the range an assistant is most likely to lift verbatim.

2 · Structured data

An identical Person graph on every page carries the four verified identifiers in sameAs, joined by @id references so the claims are provably about the same entity.

3 · FAQPage markup

The question and answer pairs below are mirrored in FAQPage JSON-LD so a search result can show them inline.

4 · Crawler permissions

robots.txt names every major AI crawler explicitly — GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, CopilotBot and others — rather than relying on a wildcard, and points at the sitemap.

5 · Machine-readable text

llms.txt gives a compact index and llms-full.txt the full text, following the llmstxt.org convention, so an assistant can ingest the site without scraping.

Crawl hygiene

One canonical URL per page, a unique title and description, a 404 page, an XML sitemap listing only canonical pages, and no duplicate rel attributes that would be silently dropped by the parser.
