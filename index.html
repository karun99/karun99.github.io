Who is Sai Karun Nandipati?

Sai Karun Nandipati is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at PB Siddhartha College of Arts and Science in India. He is also an AI researcher and a fullstack engineer. He previously taught at KBN College.

His research focuses on Agentic AI, multi-agent systems, Generative AI, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and Human-AI Symbiosis — the study of how intelligent systems and people collaborate effectively.

Sai Karun Nandipati is the author of more than 30 books on artificial intelligence, including Agentic AI: Understanding and Building Autonomous Intelligent Systems, Foundations of RAG, Prompt Engineering, and Generative AI.

He builds open-source software, including S-AI, a multi-agent swarm-intelligence framework published on npm as @saikarun/s-ai, and Collabuild, a pipeline that turns research papers into working prototypes. He also builds live web applications used by students.
