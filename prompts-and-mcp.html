MCP SERVERS

Sai Karun Nandipati publishes 19 Model Context Protocol servers for AI applications. MCP is the open standard that lets Claude, Cursor, ChatGPT and other AI clients call external tools and data sources over a single local interface. All 19 are submitted to both the Glama.ai registry and the M8ven Trust Index; 2 are live on Glama and 4 are live-verified on M8ven today — see the indexing table below.
